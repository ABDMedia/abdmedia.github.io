Privacy Policy

All-in-One QR Scanner & Maker

Developer: ABDMedia · Effective date: November 2, 2026

In short
  • The app works fully offline and has no internet access.
  • We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data.
  • Everything you scan or create stays on your device, and you can delete it at any time.

1. Who we are

All-in-One QR Scanner & Maker ("the app") is developed by ABDMedia ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles information.

2. Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising, and it does not request internet access. We have no servers that receive data from the app.

3. Device permissions and how they are used

  • Camera: used only to scan QR codes and barcodes. Images from the camera are analysed on your device in real time and are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Photos / gallery: when you choose an image to scan, only that image is read, on your device. When you tap Save, the generated code is saved to your gallery.
  • Wi-Fi: used only when you tap Connect on a scanned Wi-Fi code, to connect your device to that network.
  • Vibration: optional feedback when a code is scanned.

4. Data stored on your device

The app keeps a history of codes you scan and create, plus your settings, in the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share or export it yourself. You can turn history off, delete single items, or clear all history in the app. Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

5. Sharing

When you use Share or Export, the content is passed to the app or destination you choose, using Android's standard share sheet. That transfer is under your control and is governed by the receiving app's privacy policy.

6. Third-party components

Barcode recognition uses Google ML Kit with an on-device model bundled in the app; it runs locally and the app sends no data to Google. Code generation uses the open-source ZXing library, also on your device.

7. Links opened from scanned codes

If a scanned code contains a web link and you choose to open it, it opens in your browser. Websites you visit have their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

8. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Security

Because no data is transmitted or stored by us, there is no data on our side that could be exposed. Data on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox.

10. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how information is handled (for example, by adding advertising), we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.

11. Contact
