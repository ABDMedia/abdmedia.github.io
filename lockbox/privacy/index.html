Privacy Policy

LockBox

Developer: ABDMedia · Effective date: November 10, 2026

In short
  • LockBox has no accounts or analytics. The current version has no ads; future versions may show ads from Google AdMob (see section 6).
  • We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data.
  • Your PIN or pattern, your recovery question and your list of locked apps stay on your device.

1. Who we are

LockBox ("the app") is developed by ABDMedia ("we", "us"). It lets you protect chosen apps with a PIN, a pattern or your phone's fingerprint or face unlock. This policy explains how the app handles information.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any data ourselves. The app has no user accounts and no analytics, and we have no servers that receive data from the app. The current version contains no advertising and does not request internet access. If ads are added, the ad provider may collect some data, as described in section 6.

3. Device permissions and how they are used

  • Usage access: lets the app notice which app is opening, so it can show your lock screen when a locked app opens. This information is used on your device only, at that moment, and is not stored or shared.
  • Display over other apps: lets the app show its lock screen on top of a locked app.
  • List of installed apps: used to show the apps you can choose to lock, and to offer to lock an app the first time you open it or after you install it.
  • Background service and notifications: a background service keeps your apps locked. Android requires it to show an ongoing notification. If notifications are allowed, the app also alerts you if protection stops, for example because a permission was turned off.
  • Run at startup: restarts protection after your phone restarts.
  • Battery optimization exemption (optional): asks Android not to close the app in the background, so your apps stay locked.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock (optional, off by default): handled entirely by Android. The app only learns whether the check succeeded and never receives your fingerprint or face data.
  • Device administrator (optional, for Uninstall protection): used only to stop the app from being uninstalled without your PIN or pattern. The app uses no other administrator abilities: it cannot lock, wipe or monitor your device.

4. Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your device:

  • Your PIN or pattern, stored only as a salted, one-way hash, so it cannot be read back.
  • Your recovery question and answer, if you set one. The answer is stored as a salted hash, and also encrypted with a key held by Android's secure key storage so that you can view it after entering your PIN or pattern.
  • The list of apps you have locked, and your settings.

This data is excluded from device backups and never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. Your PIN or pattern, recovery answer and list of locked apps are never shared with anyone, including advertisers. The Rate us option opens the app's page in your device's app store, which has its own privacy policy.

6. Advertising

The current version of LockBox shows no ads. Future versions may display ads provided by Google AdMob and its advertising partners. When ads are shown, AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, general location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, in order to show and measure ads. This is governed by Google's policy on how it uses data from partner apps and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required by law, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app will ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device's settings. LockBox never gives advertisers your PIN or pattern, your recovery answer, your list of locked apps or which apps you open.

7. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is not directed at children, and if ads are added, they will be configured in line with Google's policies for apps not directed at children.

8. Security

Because we do not receive or store any data from the app, there is no data on our side that could be exposed. Data on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox, and your PIN or pattern is never stored in readable form.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how information is handled , we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
